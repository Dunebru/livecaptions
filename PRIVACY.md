# Privacy Policy for Live Captions

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Live Captions transcribes audio playing on your Mac or from the microphone, on device. It downloads the speech model once from Hugging Face; Apple's Translation framework downloads language packs from Apple. Audio is never stored or sent anywhere.

**Data collection.** Live Captions does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

**Files you open.** Files stay on your device. Anything the app produces is written only where you choose to save it.

**Permissions.** Any system permission the app asks for (such as microphone, screen recording, camera, or Apple Health) is used only for the feature you turned on, and only while you use it.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised at https://github.com/Dunebru/livecaptions/issues.
